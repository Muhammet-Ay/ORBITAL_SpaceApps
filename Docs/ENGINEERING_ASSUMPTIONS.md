# Engineering assumptions

ORBITAL uses a simplified educational model. The rocket equation estimates staged delta-v. A simplified Earth–Moon transfer estimates lunar arrival and orbit insertion. Ascent losses use a fixed allowance, and the flight view replays the model's event list with compressed display time.

Solar generation, eclipse battery use, contact time, and data storage are budget estimates. Part performance, prices in M credits, science weights, and scoring thresholds are gameplay values. NASA reference constants and the model's limitations are documented in the [v2.1 engineering formulas](CLAUDE_RESEARCH/ENGINEERING_FORMULAS.md).
